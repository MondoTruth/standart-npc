# standart-npc Changelog

## 1.0.0
Initial release.
- Added Warper, Buffer, and Stylist NPCs.

## 2.0.0
- Added Reset Girl (skill reset, stat reset, or both).
- Added Job Master (job changer).
- Added Kafra Employee (save point and storage).
- Added Card Exchanger (10 cards → Old Card Album, 30 cards → Mystical Card Album).

## 3.0.0
- Added Tool Dealer NPC in Prontera (143, 178).

## 3.1.0
- Job Master: now grants the appropriate equipment on job change.
- Buffer: removed the dialog window — clicking the NPC now heals and buffs instantly.
- Buffer: added sound effects/animations for the heal and each buff (Blessing, Agi Up, Assumptio, Kyrie Eleison, Magnificat).

## 3.1.1
- Job Master: fixed Ranger job change not granting the Wolf Flute — the third-class reward switch had a Sniper Goggle line for Hunter-line characters but no Wolf Flute alongside it (item ID 6124, confirmed against the server's own item_db). 

## 3.2
- Job Master: replaced the script with rAthena's upstream fix from pull request #9742 ( https://github.com/rathena/rathena/pull/9742 ), which properly resolves "can't change into 4th job using jobchanger" (issue #9733) plus related fixes for Kagerou/Oboro → Shinkiro/Shiranui routing and mount/dragon/Mado Gear/Warg/homunculus checks. Location and sprite kept as prontera,157,195,5 / 866. The Wolf Flute reward (item 6124) for Ranger was re-applied on top, since the upstream file doesn't include it.

## 3.3
- Buffer: added Soul Linker's Spirit (Soul Link) buff, matched to the player's class (falls back to SL_HIGH for classes without a dedicated spirit), plus Kaupe, Kaahi, and Kaizel. Fixed a real bug from the earlier attempt — BaseJob is a bare pseudo-variable in rAthena, not a function; "BaseJob(Class())" was invalid syntax and broke the whole script's compile, which is why nothing buffed at all. Sprite changed to 936 across the base NPC and all town duplicates.
- All 8 NPCs: cleaned up script comments — replaced version-history notes with short "how to customize" instructions (what to edit for prices, assortment, buff duration, destinations, etc.).

## 3.4
- Kafra Employee: added a free Identify option — identifies every unidentified item in your inventory in one click.
- Replaced Tool Dealer with Smuggler (same spot, prontera 143,178). "Regular goods" opens the same fixed shop as before. "Something special" lets the player type any item ID and buy it at a 2x markup, no restrictions — any item in item_db is purchasable this way. Items with no Buy price in item_db (a real item, just 0z listed) get a fixed 5000z fallback before the markup; a completely invalid ID is still refused.

## 3.5
- Split Tool Dealer and Smuggler back into two separate NPCs. Tool Dealer is a plain fixed shop again, same spot. Smuggler now only does buy-any-item-by-ID special orders, moved next to Tool Dealer (prontera 144,174).
- Added 4 new NPCs by Lil Art: Safe Refiner, Overlook Fisherman, Costume Stone Enchanter, and Eden Group Gramps turn-in.
- Author credit updated: MondoTruth, Lil Art, Claude, and the Ragnarok Offline community.
- Buffer: added Soul Link, Poem of Bragi, Kaupe, and Kaizel. Heal now applies after every buff, so it accounts for any HP increase from them.
- Job Master: fixed a bug where changing straight to 3rd class without rebirthing first could break the character. Only Rebirth is offered until the character has actually transcended.

## 3.5.1
- Gramps and Overlook: cut permanent monster spawns from 5,000 to 500 total. They were script-spawned (not covered by dynamic_mobs) and stayed alive server-wide 24/7, causing a real measured CPU/lag hit for every player.

## 3.6
- Gramps and Overlook: real fix for 3.5.1's stopgap. Spawns moved out of OnInit script calls into dedicated map spawn files (gramps_mobs.txt, overlook_mobs.txt), so dynamic_mobs now actually governs them -- monsters only exist while a player is on the map, instead of permanently, server-wide. Kill-quest tracking unaffected. Spawn count kept at 50 (from 3.5.1) rather than reverted to the original 500/250.

## 3.7
- Gramps: spawn count raised from 50 to 100 per monster type (still dynamic_mobs-governed, so this doesn't bring back the permanence problem).
- Smuggler: special-order pricing changed to a flat 10,000,000z, replacing the old fallback+markup logic.

## 3.8
- Smuggler: special-order pricing now split by item type. Cards, gear, and consumables get a flat 10,000,000z. Everything else gets a 1000z fallback (for items with no listed Buy price) plus a x5 markup.
- Card Exchanger: added a card extraction option — pick an equipped item slot and pull the first card out of it.
- All 14 NPCs: cleaned up script comments into short "how to customize" notes.
- Heads up: the app's new monster spawn-rate setting also scales Gramps' and Overlook's dungeon spawns, same as any other map spawn.

## 3.9
- Gramps: bounty progress now tracked per character instead of per account (breaking change -- anyone with an in-progress bounty starts over). Players who level past a bracket can still turn in an already-active bounty. Real-time kill progress now posts to chat.
- Overlook: same three changes applied -- per-character progress (also breaking), can still enter to finish an active bounty past Base Level 85, kill progress posts to chat.
- Gramps: added 3 more level brackets -- 151-175 (verus03), 176-200 (ein_dun03), 201+ (nif_dun01), same 400-kill/2-monster structure as before, with scaling EXP.
- Big thanks to IceGlaive for this whole batch of contributions -- the per-character migration, over-level bypass, live kill notifications, and the three new brackets with server-verified monster IDs are all his work.

## 4.0
- Gramps: bounties above your level now cost 25% EXP instead of being blocked outright, unless you started on-level and simply outleveled it mid-hunt. Bounties can now be abandoned.
- Gramps: split the old 201+ bracket into 201-230 and a new 231+ (nif_dun02).
- Added Master Nokzin (prontera 164,166) and Assistant Denrik (prontera 164,163) -- weapon elemental endows.
- Thanks to IceGlaive (bounty penalty/abandon system), Lil Art (the 231+ bracket), and Nokzin (Master Nokzin/Assistant Denrik) for this release.

## 4.1
- Added Cheffenia MVP Dungeon (prontera 140,180) -- timed access to a full MVP roster, paid in Zeny or Cash Points.
- Added Illusion Dungeon bounties (prontera 147,166) -- same style as Gramps/Overlook, across 9 Illusion dungeons.
- Added Weight Maxxer (prontera 171,180) -- trades a Gym Pass for +1 permanent carry capacity, up to 10 times.
- Thanks to Lil Art for this whole batch.

## 4.2.0
- Removed Assistant Denrik -- Elemental Converter scrolls are already available through the Cash Shop, a more convenient option.
- Added Prontera Training Dummies (Medium & Large) -- first version, will be refined further.
- Costume Stone Enchanter: now also enchants the Costume Garment slot, and checks item type directly instead of an ID range.
- Thanks to Lil Art for this whole batch.

## 4.3.0
- Reorganized into individual checkboxes, now that npc/when/<setting> is supported by the app: a core that's always on, everything else toggled one by one.
- Requires app version 1.3.2 or newer.
- Gramps: EXP rewards for brackets 126+ roughly tripled, to balance out against the newly added Illusion bounties. By IceGlaive.

## 4.4.0
- Fixed the enchanter. Now enchants all stones and similar items correctly.
- Added Blacksmith Blessing to the Tool Dealer in Prontera.
- Introduced the Episode Clearing Valkyrie that reads episode clear tickets.

## 4.4.1
- Gramps: over-level bounty penalty reduced -- you now keep 75% of the reward instead of 25%, to better balance against Illusion and MVP rewards.
- Gramps: fixed the 231+ bracket -- its hunt target changed to Corrupted Grote and Corrupted Blue Moon Loli Ruri (nif_dun02), and the kill tracker now has its own separate check matching the new monsters. Bounty was previously uncompletable after the monster swap; now fixed.
- Gramps: the 101-125 bracket's hunt target changed from Kaho & Flame Shooter to Nightmare Terror & Blazer.
- Thanks to IceGlaive for the penalty rebalance and the 231+ bracket fix.

## 4.5.0
- Added Lucky John (prontera 138,172) -- daily Mercenary Guild area-purge contracts: kill any 500 monsters on your assigned map. 9 level brackets from Lv 30 to 231+, one contract per day per character.
- Smuggler: now detects the server era automatically. On Renewal, Healing items moved off the flat 10,000,000z price onto the normal item price x5. On Pre-renewal the flat price is gone entirely -- every item costs its normal price x2 (2,000z if the item has no price).
- Thanks to IceGlaive for Lucky John.

## 4.6.0
- Buffer now has its own checkbox in Settings → Mods, so it can be turned off like the other NPCs. On by default.
- Gramps, Lucky John and Illusion Manager now take their rewards from one shared file, so they can be balanced side by side.
- EXP rewards rebalanced: late-game bounties (Gramps and Lucky John at Lv 201+, Illusion's Underwater 2F) now give many times more EXP, so they feel worth the time even on 1x rates. Early and mid-level bounties give less than before. Lucky John also pays more Zeny.
- Gramps: no more 75% penalty for taking a bounty above your level -- you always get the full reward.
- Gramps: fixed a formatting error that kept the Lv 151-175 map (verus03) from getting its map setting.
- Thanks to IceGlaive for the shared rewards system and the EXP rebalance.
