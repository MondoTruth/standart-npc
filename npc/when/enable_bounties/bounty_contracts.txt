//===== Description: =========================================
//= Every contract the Bounty Hunter offers, one line each.
//= Replaces config_shared_rewards.txt: the reward now sits on
//= the same line as the monsters it is paid for.
//= bounty_board.txt reads this once at server start.
//===== How to customize: ====================================
//= One line = one contract:
//=   "type|min lv|max lv|map|warp x,y|monster IDs|kills|base|job|zeny"
//= - type: 1 = Target Hunt (was Gramps), 2 = Overlook Water
//=   Dungeon, 3 = Illusion Dungeons, 4 = Daily Area Purge (was
//=   Lucky John).
//= - max lv 999 = no upper limit. The minimum is enforced; above
//=   the maximum the player may still take it. Overlook (type 2)
//=   ignores these and uses one range for the whole dungeon
//=   (.owd_min_lv/.owd_max_lv in bounty_board.txt); its 4 lines
//=   must stay in floor order.
//= - map: for types 1-3 the hunting map; for type 4 a
//=   comma-separated pool, one is picked at random.
//= - warp x,y: where the "go there" option drops the player
//=   (0,0 = no warp, as for type 4).
//= - monster IDs: up to 3, comma-separated (type 4: 0 = any
//=   monster on the map). Only monsters from bounty_mobs.txt
//=   count for types 1-3, so add their spawn lines there too.
//= - kills: needed per monster (type 4: in total).
//= - base / job: EXP in units of 10,000 (450 = 4,500,000), then
//=   scaled by the bounty EXP rate. zeny: units of 50,000.
//= - Several maps for the same levels: just add another line.
//= - Order matters only for moving over old progress from the
//=   Gramps/Overlook/Illusion/Lucky John NPCs: keep the first
//=   lines of each type in their old order.
//============================================================
