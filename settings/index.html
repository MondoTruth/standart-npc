Switch off what you don't want in your world. Changes take effect after Save and restart server. A switched-off NPC is removed from the world; players' progress with it stays on their characters.

Buffer — choose buffs

Everything ticked is cast on click; the full heal always comes last. Buffs marked ⚠ cancel each other in the game, so only one of them can be on.

3rd and 4th class buffs work on Renewal only. 4th class buffs raise P.ATK, S.MATK, RES, MRES, C.RATE and the trait stats (POW, STA, WIS, SPL, CON, CRT), which matter mostly for 4th classes; for other characters they do little.

Warper — options

Both are on by default. Each one applies to every Warper in every town, and to Last Warp.

Stylist — prices

The Stylist opens the styling window; a player pays when pressing Buy there, in Zeny or with a coupon (New Style Coupon for a hair style, Clothing Dye Coupon for a cloth color, Costume Change Ticket for the outfit; a hair color is always Zeny). A change of several things at once is paid in full or not made at all. The outfit (standard or alternative, 3rd and 4th classes) is Renewal only.

Smuggler — banned goods

Banned item IDs Item IDs separated by commas, spaces or new lines, e.g. 4121, 4123. Find an ID in game with @iteminfo <name>.

Attendance Check — rewards

One reward a day, sent by mail; after day 20 the list starts over. Leave a day empty for the mod's own reward (shown under the day). An item the server does not know also keeps the mod's reward, and the server log says so. Find an ID in game with @iteminfo <name>.
